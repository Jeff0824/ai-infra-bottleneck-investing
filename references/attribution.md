## What was taken

The **method** only:

- invest in physical bottleneck layers rather than AI applications
- the three-part neocloud durability test (finances, chips, energy)
- the operational definition of quality (grew through the drawdown, no distressed raise)
- the uniform-decline-plus-flat-volume test for forced selling
- the requirement to distinguish a multiple floor from an earnings floor
- dated catalysts and forced-selling-end mechanics
